Model & method — comps-odds v1

implied = TVL × comp × odds × growth

  • comp — the category's median market-cap/TVL among its token-bearing protocols (5 or more peers, else the global median 0.241), clamped to 0.05–0.60. A launch is priced like its peers.
  • odds — the category's token rate (share of protocols in the category that already record a token), clamped to 0.20–0.80, times a maturity factor 0.70–1.00 over four years of tracking. Unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • growth — 1 + clamped 7-day TVL change (−50%…+100%) × 0.5.

What counts as a launch

A protocol is ranked only if it records no token and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades: no Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC or CEX category, no wrapped/staked/staking name, no exchange prefix, no version marker. That is the house watchlist's own rule, applied to all 198 ranked rows. WBTC, Binance staked ETH and USDT0 are never ranked as launches.

Limits — read before trusting the board

  • The snapshot is frozen at 14 Nov 2026. Nothing after that date is in it.
  • Market cap here is circulating market cap, not fully diluted value; comps inherit that.
  • The category token rate is a crude proxy for launch odds. It says nothing about a team's plans.
  • “Records no token” means DefiLlama records none — a protocol may still have one.
  • Farm plans are generic by category, not advice. Never pay to claim an airdrop.
